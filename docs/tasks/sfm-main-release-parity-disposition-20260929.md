# 4.34.0 exploratory parity disposition, 29 September 2026

Decision: the checked-in `released-4.34.0` projects are a sufficiently faithful baseline for continued **exploratory source-projection development** on this branch. This is a bounded engineering decision, not approval to publish their JARs, replace a default branch, promote a future preset, or claim general gameplay compatibility. The [candidate matrix](sfm-main-release-candidate-matrix-20260928.md) records the exact official and projected production-JAR SHA-256 values used by the comparisons, including the later controlled 1.20.2 rebuild. Do not substitute the opt-in RegexCache candidate for this released baseline.

The [candidate matrix](sfm-main-release-candidate-matrix-20260928.md#remaining-production-runtime-parity-gates) is the detailed evidence ledger, including exact loader/JDK identities, hashes, shared warnings and unrun cells. The [source-projection plan](sfm-main-source-projection-plan.md) retains SP-05's wider release-compatibility gate. When a real `released-X` candidate is chosen, its own immutable inputs, differences and runtime evidence need separate review; this disposition does not transfer approval to it.

The 26.1.2 packaged-client cell has since passed a separate exact-loader test-only bridge (commit `cd2b2d8e1`): official and projected production JARs each rendered a title screen and a synchronized, ray-hit manager in a flat world. The pinned installer's offline client-patch processor reproduced the cached client byte-for-byte; all four result files and screenshots were independently rehashed and visually reviewed. This closes the earlier *unrun* cell only for startup, sync and rendering, not arbitrary client gameplay or modded interactions. The detailed path-free hashes and shared warnings are in the [candidate matrix](sfm-main-release-candidate-matrix-20260928.md).

The exact 1.20.2 packaged-client cell has since passed a separate four-run test-only bridge (commit `d95d49648`): official and projected production JARs each rendered a complete NeoForge 20.2.86 title menu and a synchronized, ray-hit manager in a fresh flat world. The Mojang asset index `8` (SHA-1 `21beaec863755c8fd4620b22ed9bdbc6718b3c32`) and all its objects were verified in isolated scratch, and the installer pipeline twice produced the same readable patched client at SHA-256 `0bebc71df20aadba9d7ee56ad5dbd92c87546d7fd0e29397cb637a1f9fd5dc94`. That patched digest is scratch repeatability evidence, not an independently published artifact match. All four result/PNG hashes and screenshots were independently checked; the detailed path-free evidence and shared warnings are in the [candidate matrix](sfm-main-release-candidate-matrix-20260928.md). This closes only bounded startup, replication and rendering, not arbitrary gameplay or modded interactions.

The [server-config comparison](sfm-main-server-config-parity-20260929.md) finds equal `sfm-server.toml` bytes for all ten official/projected pairs. Only 1.20.4, 1.21.0, 1.21.1 and 26.1.2 generated those files independently in each boot; the six older pairs inherited the official seed world's server config. This is one bounded config-file comparison, not proof that each setting behaves the same or that user-edited configs migrate.

The test-only [server witness](../../scripts/release-server-witness/README.md) also compared the released `sfm` command subtree and SFM network-registration topology in fresh official/projected pairs at the Forge 1.19.2 and NeoForge 26.1.2 endpoints. Each command pair has 14 matching nodes; each network pair has 37 matching registrations under its exact pinned loader. The final network snapshots include exact target/loader identity and codec-presence fields. These comparisons do not execute commands, evaluate permission predicates, encode/decode packets, or prove delivery. Their path-free report hashes and regression-smoke limits are recorded in the [candidate matrix](sfm-main-release-candidate-matrix-20260928.md#remaining-production-runtime-parity-gates).
